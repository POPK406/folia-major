Custom Electron schemes must be registered in the single `protocol.registerSchemesAsPrivileged` call at startup — a second call silently overwrites prior privileges. Wallpaper mode requires external binaries found via `FOLIA_WINDOWTOLAYER_PATH` / `FOLIA_WALLPAPER_HELPER_PATH` overrides or under `process.resourcesPath`; missing binaries gracefully disable the feature. Linux packaging sets `ELECTRON_LINUX_PACKAGED_GRAPHICS=true` and uses `FOLIA_LINUX_GRAPHICS_MODE` to select `system`/`swiftshader`/`software` rendering paths.
